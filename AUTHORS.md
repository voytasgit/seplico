# Authors and origin

## Original concept and specification

**Original concept and specification: Vadym Voytas.**

Vadym Voytas conceived the Seplico project and authored the initial Seplico Specification in 2026.

The defining principle of the project is:

> **An application identifies an application - not the person behind it.**

Seplico was developed around the idea that professional information for one job application can initially be represented separately from direct identity, followed by a distinct and consent-based identity-release step when an employer wants to continue.
